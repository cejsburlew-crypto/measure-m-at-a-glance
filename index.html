FMP Guidelines — how to read the budgets above against how we are actually delivering the work
1
The FMP was estimated on Design-Bid-Build, not Lease-Leaseback. Every High / Medium / Low total above carries DBB pricing assumptions. Projects delivered LLB should be expected to land higher per square foot — not because the building changed, but because the delivery method prices risk differently.
Delivery Method Expected LA County DSA Cost
new STEM / classroom		 $550 — $1,050+ / SF
Design-Bid-Build (DBB) FMP basis $600–800 / SF
Lease-Leaseback — Typical Market $650–900 / SF
Lease-Leaseback — Heavy Risk Loading Current LLB Contractor Pool $750–1,000+ / SF
2
Risk loading is the variable that moves the number. The question behind every Lease-Leaseback proposal is simply: how much uncertainty is each proposer asking the District to pay for? Unresolved scope, escalation, labor availability, subcontractor market, and schedule risk all get priced into the GMP as contingency. That premium — not the physical building — is what carries a $600–800/SF project into the $750–1,000+/SF band. Where a proposal lands in that range tells you how much risk is being transferred to the District up front versus retained and managed by the builder.
3
What this does to FMP buying power. At the midpoints — $700/SF DBB versus $875/SF heavily loaded LLB — the same dollar buys roughly 20% less scope. An FMP tier that was sized to deliver a full scope list under DBB should be read as delivering about four-fifths of that list if the work goes out as heavily risk-loaded Lease-Leaseback.
